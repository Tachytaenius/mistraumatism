 M I S T R A U M A T I S M
  by Tachytaenius

Mistraumatism is a top-down CP437 text graphics game about pain and fear.
The player's character wakes up in a facility filled with monsters and has to shoot their way out.

If you wish, project your own painful feelings into the violent horror of the game. If you escape, you can hold that sense of relief for your self and the feelings you poured in. That is its purpose.
If you die trying, then you are reminded that you were only attempting to complete a game. It is a coping tool, not a test of "worth".

It is not a power fantasy. It is a survival fantasy.


It is written (with love) for the LÖVE framework, version 11.5.

The soundtrack source can be found at https://github.com/Tachytaenius/mistraumatism-soundtrack
